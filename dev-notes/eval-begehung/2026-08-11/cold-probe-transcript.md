## Returned verbatim

1. Expected review behavior/method: A systematic physical-style
walkthrough — the reviewer moves through the target
(system/corpus/codebase/site) along a defined route or checklist,
area by area, rather than jumping straight to a known problem spot.
I'd expect: a fixed traversal order covering the whole object, a
checklist or set of criteria checked at each stop, findings logged
per area as encountered, and a closing protocol/report listing what
was checked and what was found — deficiencies plus (often) required
follow-up actions. The emphasis is on not skipping segments, i.e.
coverage discipline over depth-first investigation of one lead.

2. Established German professional practice evoked: "Begehung" is
the standard term for a site/premises walkthrough-inspection in
several regulated German professional contexts — most centrally
Arbeitsschutz (occupational safety, e.g. Sicherheitsbegehung /
Arbeitsstättenbegehung — a legally-flavored, often mandated periodic
inspection of a workplace), and similarly Brandschutzbegehung
(fire-safety walkthrough) and Baustellenbegehung /
Bauabnahmebegehung (construction-site walkthrough/handover
inspection). Defining features of that practice: (a) a qualified
inspector physically traverses the entire premises rather than
sampling; (b) it follows a checklist or defined route; (c) it is
typically periodic/recurring rather than one-off (an "erste" and
then "regelmäßige" Begehung); (d) it produces a formal written
protocol (Begehungsprotokoll) — findings, deficiencies, responsible
parties, deadlines for correction; (e) it carries an
accountability/audit-trail character — often tied to legal or
insurance obligations, with signatures.

3. Scope/scheduling/documentation connotations, from the name
alone:
- Scope: yes — connotes whole-object coverage. A "Begehung" implies
  walking the entire area/route, not a spot-check or sample of one
  part. Partial coverage would undercut the word's own claim.
- Scheduling/recurrence: yes — strongly connotes a periodic,
  repeatable event (regelmäßige Begehung), not a single ad-hoc
  pass. The term carries an expectation of returning on a cadence.
- Documentation form: yes — connotes a structured,
  checklist/protocol-shaped written record (Begehungsprotokoll)
  rather than free-form prose — findings tied to specific stops on
  the route, often with an explicit "checked / not checked" or
  pass/fail marking per item.
